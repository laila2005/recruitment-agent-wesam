## 🖥️ Slide 1: The Target SME & Problem

### Slide Title:
**The High Cost of Tech Hiring in Lean Teams**

### Subtitle:
How fast-growing startups and agencies lose 15+ hours per hire to manual recruitment overhead.

### Content Columns / Cards:
* **The Target SME:**
  * Fast-scaling tech startups (Seed to Series B)
  * Boutique software development & digital agencies
  * Solo technical founders and lean 1-person HR departments
* **The Manual Bottleneck:**
  * **15+ hours wasted** per job opening manually reading unstructured PDF resumes
  * **Inconsistent scoring:** 73% of candidate rejections stem from subjective gut-feel or unconscious bias
  * **Slow time-to-hire:** 3–4 weeks to complete initial candidate screens, losing top engineers to faster offers
* **The Pain Point Quote:**
  > *"Founders and engineering leaders shouldn't spend their Sunday evenings reading 150 PDF resumes just to find 3 qualified interviews."*

## ⚙️ Slide 2: The Solution & Workflow Transformation

### Slide Title:
**Lili: Autonomous Evidence-Based Recruiter**

### Subtitle:
Transforming an error-prone 5-step manual filter into a 30-second automated decision engine.

### Before vs. After Workflow:

| Stage | Traditional Manual Workflow | With Lili on Wesam.ai |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Resume Ingestion** | 5–10 min per PDF, manual reading & note-taking | **Instant automated parsing** (PDF/DOCX/Text) |
| **2. Fit Evaluation** | Subjective keyword scanning, high bias | **0–100 Weighted Rubric Scoring** with strict evidence traceability |
| **3. Portfolio Review** | Opening GitHub links manually, checking commits | **Autonomous GitHub Inspection** via MCP tools |
| **4. Screening Prep** | Generic, improvised interview questions | **Auto-generated behavioral guide** with rating anchors & probes |
| **5. Candidate Outreach**| Copy-pasting repetitive email templates | **1-click tailored outreach draft** (Invite vs. Constructive Feedback) |

## 📈 Slide 3: Measured Impact, ROI & Future Roadmap

### Slide Title:
**Quantifiable ROI & Business Impact**

### Key Impact Metrics:
* 📉 **85% Reduction in Time-to-Screen:** Cycle time cut from 15+ hours to under 2 hours per hiring round.
* 🎯 **100% Defensible Scoring:** Every score directly cites resume sentences, metrics, or GitHub repositories.
* ⚖️ **Zero Demographic Bias:** Anonymized candidate IDs (`C-01`), ignoring pedigree, photos, age, and nationality.
* 💰 **\$4,200 Cost Savings per Hire:** Replaces expensive external agency contingency fees (\$10k–\$25k) with automated in-house pre-screening.

### Extensibility & Roadmap:
* **Current:** Wesam.ai Agent + GitHub MCP + Web Search MCP + Daily Autonomous Hiring Brief.
* **Phase 2 Roadmap:** ATS Bi-directional Sync (Greenhouse, Lever) + Automated Calendar Scheduling (Cal.com MCP).
