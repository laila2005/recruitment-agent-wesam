## 🖥️ Slide 1: The Problem — The Startup Hiring Paradox

### Slide Title:
**Big-Company Recruiting Expectations on a Zero-Dollar ATS Budget**

### Subtitle:
How resource-constrained startups and agencies waste 15+ hours per hiring cycle without access to enterprise HR tools.

### Visual Comparison Cards:
* **The Enterprise Reality:**
  * Uses \$10k/yr Greenhouse/Lever + \$5k/yr Gem outreach + full-time recruiting teams.
* **The SME Reality:**
  * Zero budget for ATS software.
  * Inboxes flooded with 150+ unstructured PDF resumes.
  * Founders spend Sunday nights manually copying data into messy Google Sheets.
  * Top talent slips away due to 3-week screening delays.
  * Candidates get ghosted because writing 50 personalized rejection emails is impossible.

### The Pain Metric:
* **15+ Hours** wasted per cycle on resume scanning, manual tracking, and email drafting.
* **\$15,000+** wasted if forced to rely on external recruiting agencies.

## ⚙️ Slide 2: The Solution — The Autonomous "Zero-Dollar ATS"

### Slide Title:
**Lili: Replacing the Entire \$17k/yr Recruiting Software Stack**

### Subtitle:
An autonomous recruiting agent that turns any inbox into an evidence-based, bias-free talent engine.

### The Replaced Workflow:

| Enterprise Tool | Replaced Function | How Lili Solves It Autonomously |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greenhouse / Lever** (\$10,000/yr) | Resume Parsing & Pipeline Database | **Multimodal PDF/Word Ingestion** ➔ Instant structured CSV export for Google Sheets/Notion. |
| **HackerRank / Screen** (\$2,500/yr) | Technical Vetting & Evaluation | **Weighted 0–100 Rubric Scoring** with strict evidence traceability and score caps. |
| **Gem / Outreach** (\$4,500/yr) | Candidate Communication | **1-Click Personalized Emails** (Fast-track invites, keep-warm updates, and respectful rejections). |
| **Screening Calls** (15 hrs manual) | Interview Prep & Note-Taking | **Auto-Generated Behavioral Guides** with calibrated rating anchors (1–4) targeting specific resume gaps. |

## 📈 Slide 3: Measured Impact, Real ROI & Market Viability

### Slide Title:
**Quantifiable ROI & Scalable Market Impact**

### Key Impact Numbers:
* 📉 **85% Reduction in Time-to-Screen:** End-to-end evaluation cycle compressed from 15+ hours to under 2 hours.
* 💵 **\$17,000/year Saved on SaaS Licenses:** Zero ATS subscription cost required for early-stage companies.
* ⚖️ **100% Bias-Mitigated Screening:** Default candidate anonymization (`Candidate C-01`), eliminating demographic and pedigree biases.
* ✉️ **Zero Candidate Ghosting:** 100% of applicants receive tailored status updates or constructive feedback, protecting employer brand.

### Operational Proof & Extensibility:
* **Live on Wesam.ai:** gpt-5.5 model, multi-skill routing, persistent JD reference memory.
* **Autonomous Employee Behavior:** Daily scheduled web-search workflow briefing founders on live salary and tech stack trends at 8:00 AM.
* **Connected Ecosystem:** GitMCP tool integration for inspecting live candidate repositories directly.
