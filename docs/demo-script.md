# 2–3 Minute Demo Video Script: Lili (TalentScout AI)

> **Hackathon Submission:** Agents at Work (1st Edition)  
> **Target Video Length:** 2:30 minutes  
> **Screen Setup:** Split screen or Full screen on Wesam.ai Preview tab + GitHub Repo tab.

---

## 🎬 Video Breakdown & Voiceover

### [0:00 – 0:30] Introduction & The Problem
* **On Screen:** Title slide or Wesam Builder overview screen showing Lili's profile.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Hi everyone, this is Lili, our autonomous Technical Recruiter Agent built on Wesam.ai for the Agents at Work Hackathon. Lean tech startups and software agencies spend over 15 hours every hiring cycle manually sifting through PDF resumes, dealing with subjective bias, and writing repetitive interview questions. Lili automates this entire pipeline with evidence-based rigor."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Core Live Demo: Resume-to-Job Fit Evaluation
* **On Screen:** Switch to **Preview tab**. Show uploading or pasting the Senior Backend Engineer resume (`Alex Chen`).
* **Action:** Send message: *"Evaluate this resume for the Senior Backend Engineer role."*
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Here, we upload a candidate's resume against our target Senior Backend Engineer job description, which is persistently stored as a reference file in Wesam. Notice how Lili doesn't just summarize: she anonymizes the candidate to eliminate demographic bias, maps evidence directly to role criteria, and calculates an objective weighted fit score."*
* **Highlight:** Point cursor at the **Scorecard (83/100 or 98/100)**, the **Evidence Citations** (500k txns/day, p99 latency), and the **Gaps** (Kubernetes vs ECS).

---

### [1:15 – 1:45] Automated Interview Guide & Outreach Draft
* **On Screen:** Scroll down to the **Verification Questions** and **Candidate Outreach Draft**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"What truly sets Lili apart is actionable screening intelligence. Instead of generic HR questions, Lili generated candidate-specific technical probes asking how the candidate measured their 40% latency reduction and what PostgreSQL query bottlenecks they resolved. She also auto-drafts the personalized invitation email, saving hiring managers hours of manual drafting."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:10] Agent Capabilities: MCP Tools & Autonomous Workflow
* **On Screen:** Click on the **Build tab** to show GitHub MCP, Web Search MCP, and the **Daily Hiring Intelligence Brief** workflow.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Lili is equipped with real MCP tools—including GitMCP for inspecting repositories directly and Web Search for live salary benchmarks. Even better, she isn't just a chatbot: she runs an autonomous morning workflow that searches live market data to brief the team on top in-demand skills and trending salary benchmarks."*

---

### [2:10 – 2:30] Conclusion & ROI
* **On Screen:** Quick look at the GitHub repository (`README.md` and architecture docs).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"By cutting pre-screening time by 85% and standardizing evaluations across candidates, Lili turns 15 hours of hiring chaos into defensible, repeatable recruiting intelligence. Thank you!"*
