# 2–3 Minute Demo Video Script: Lili (TalentScout AI)

> **Hackathon Submission:** Agents at Work (1st Edition)  
> **Target Video Length:** 2:30 minutes  
> **Architecture Setup:** Wesam.ai (Backend Autonomous Agent) + Lovable.dev (Executive HR Dashboard)

---

## 🎬 Video Breakdown & Voiceover

### [0:00 – 0:30] Introduction & The SME Pain Point
* **On Screen:** Title slide or Wesam Builder overview screen showing Lili's profile.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Hi everyone! This is Lili, our autonomous Technical Recruiter Agent built for the Agents at Work Hackathon. Early-stage startups and lean agencies spend over 15 hours every hiring cycle manually sifting through PDF resumes, dealing with subjective bias, and writing repetitive emails. They can't afford a $15,000/year enterprise ATS like Greenhouse. Lili is their autonomous Zero-Dollar ATS."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Backend Intelligence: Wesam.ai Evaluation
* **On Screen:** Switch to **Wesam.ai Preview tab**.
* **Action:** Show candidate resume evaluation output with 6-section structure.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"On Wesam.ai, Lili runs on GPT-5.5 with modular recruiter skills. When a candidate's resume is ingested, she evaluates evidence strictly against the target Job Description stored in reference memory. Notice how she automatically anonymizes the candidate to 'Candidate C-01' to eliminate demographic bias, maps evidence directly to role criteria, enforces score caps for missing mandatory requirements, and generates candidate-specific verification probes."*
* **Highlight:** Point cursor at the **Scorecard (94/100)**, the **Evidence Citations**, and the **Autonomous Morning Workflow** running daily at 8:00 AM using Tavily Web Search.

---

### [1:15 – 2:05] Frontend Experience: Lovable.dev HR Comparison Dashboard
* **On Screen:** Switch to the **Lovable.dev Web Dashboard**!
* **Actions:**
  1. Show the **Executive KPI cards** (48 candidates screened, 14.5 hours saved, 4 Fast-Track).
  2. Toggle the **"Bias-Free Anonymize Mode"** switch on and off (showing real names convert to `Candidate C-01`).
  3. Filter by **"🟢 Tier 1: Fast-Track"** and show the visual circular fit scores (94, 78, 48).
  4. Click **"Scorecard" on Sarah Lin** to open the slide-over drawer showing her radar breakdown, interview guide, and pre-drafted invite email.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"To eliminate decision fatigue for hiring managers, Lili syncs into our executive comparison dashboard. Instead of reading 50 pages of text, founders can triage 48 applicants in 30 seconds across three clear tiers: Fast-Track, Bench, and Archive. With one toggle, we can turn on Bias-Free Anonymize Mode. Clicking any candidate reveals their full evidence-backed scorecard, customized interview guide, and a ready-to-dispatch outreach email."*

---

### [2:05 – 2:30] Conclusion, ROI & GitHub Architecture
* **On Screen:** Quick switch to GitHub repository (`README.md` and `docs/`).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"By cutting pre-screening time by 85% and replacing expensive enterprise ATS software with an autonomous agent pipeline, Lili empowers lean teams to hire top engineering talent with zero bias. All prompts, skills, and architectures are open-source on our GitHub repo. Thank you!"*
